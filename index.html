About Me

My name is Thabiso Sesiu, an Information Technology graduate, programmer, computer teacher, footballer and athlete. I was born and raised in the beautiful mountains of Mokhotlong, Lesotho, where my journey began with dreams, determination and a passion for achieving something meaningful.

My educational journey brought me to Limkokwing University of Creative Technology, where I studied Information Technology from August 2023 until September 2026, successfully graduating with Merit.

I am passionate about programming, technology and solving real-world problems through innovative solutions. I have worked with programming languages and technologies such as Java, Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, React, Node.js and SQL. Through academic and personal projects, I continue to develop my technical and problem-solving abilities.

Beyond technology, I am also a dedicated footballer representing LUFC in the Vodacom Premier League. Football has taught me discipline, teamwork, resilience and the importance of working towards a common goal. I am equally passionate about athletics and maintaining an active lifestyle.

I also have a passion for teaching. In 2025, I gained teaching experience at Maseru High School, where I taught Information and Communication Technology (ICT). This experience strengthened my communication skills and my ability to share technical knowledge with others.

In February 2026, I began my professional internship at the Lesotho Mounted Police Service (LMPS) Headquarters IT Office, where I have continued gaining practical experience in IT support, computer maintenance, networking, troubleshooting and other technical operations.

🎯 My Vision: To become a highly skilled IT professional, innovative programmer and technology leader, while continuing to inspire others through education, sports and personal development.

My story is one of determination, growth and ambition. From the mountains of Mokhotlong to the university environment, the classroom, the football field and the professional IT workplace, I believe that greatness is built through consistency, discipline and the courage to pursue your dreams.
